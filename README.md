# Ticketing & CRM Case Study — LÜMA, "ORBITE 2026-27" tour

Analysis of a simulated ticketing dataset for a 12-date live tour: sell-through
diagnosis, measurement of how much of the customer base is actually reachable, and a
costed CRM plan — including an honest account of what it cannot fix.

**Portfolio page:** https://nubar-mamedova.github.io/etude-de-cas-billetterie-crm/
**PDF version:** [`Nubar_Mamedova_Etude_de_cas_Billetterie_CRM.pdf`](Nubar_Mamedova_Etude_de_cas_Billetterie_CRM.pdf)

> ticketing market they model are French. This README covers the method and the findings

## Context

LÜMA is a fictional French alt-pop duo. The "ORBITE 2026-27" tour runs 12 dates across
France. The dataset is generated by code with a fixed seed (`20260804`) so the whole
analysis is reproducible: 3,927 unique buyers, their orders, and an earlier cohort
("PREMIER CERCLE 2024") used as the reactivation base.

The data is simulated, but the structure — sales channels, resellers, RFM segmentation,
fill curve — mirrors that of a real ticketing operation.

## Three findings

**1. Three dates are falling behind.** A pace index compares each date to the tour's
median fill curve at the same number of days before the show. Toulouse, Lille and
Montpellier are a combined **683 tickets behind**.

**2. Most of the customer base is unreachable.** Of 3,927 buyers, **761 are contactable
(19.4%)** — meaning **81% of the audience is out of reach**. Resellers hold **~77%** of
tickets sold and do not pass back email addresses.

**3. Reactivation has a ceiling.** The 2024 cohort leaves **528 dormant buyers**,
reactivable at an assumed 25% rate.

**The conclusion that matters:** adding up every campaign that can realistically run on
the reachable base, the CRM plan closes **8% of the 683-ticket gap**. The rest is a
distribution and paid-media problem, not a CRM one. Saying so is the result, not an
admission.

## The campaign plan

Four email/SMS campaigns, each starting from one finding and targeting a named segment.
Two principles held throughout:

- **A 10% unsolicited control group on every campaign.** Without a control group you
  measure ticketing seasonality, not campaign effect. The headline metric is incremental
  tickets versus control, not open rate.
- **An unsubscribe guardrail** tracked campaign by campaign, alongside cost per ticket.

Reactivation of the 2024 cohort runs as a **three-email sequence over three weeks**,
targeting fans who attended in 2024 and have been silent since (339 reachable contacts).

## Method

- **RFM segmentation** (recency, frequency, monetary) across the buyer base.
- **Pace index:** each date's sell-through measured against the tour median at a constant
  number of days out — so a date three months away isn't compared to one three weeks away.
- **Formula-driven forecasts** in the workbook: assumptions are the blue cells, and the
  plan recalculates when you change them.
- **Verified SQL:** the five queries in `requetes.sql` were run in SQLite and their
  results cross-checked against pandas.

**No figure in this study comes from an AI.** All of them are recomputed from the raw
CSVs. A verification pass re-derived all 28 figures quoted on the page.

## AI copy layer, under guardrails

`code/ia_redaction.py` industrialises the writing of A/B email variants for the four
campaigns. The prompt is **built by the code** from the target segment's own figures,
and five automated checks reject a variant before it ships:

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|
| Subject length | 45 characters maximum |
| Unsourced numbers | any figure absent from the injected data block → rejected |
| Compliance | unsubscribe mention required |
| Superlatives | blacklist of marketing terms |
| Test validity | one variable tested per A/B pair |

The generated variants and the check log are in
[`IA_variantes_campagnes.md`](IA_variantes_campagnes.md); raw output in
`data/ia_copy.json`.

To regenerate:

```bash
pip install anthropic pandas
export ANTHROPIC_API_KEY="sk-ant-..."
python code/ia_redaction.py
```

## Stated limitations

- Simulated data: the structure is realistic, the volumes are chosen.
- The 25% reactivation rate is an assumption, not a measurement — it is a parameter in
  the workbook.
- The model covers neither box-office sales nor peer-to-peer resale.
